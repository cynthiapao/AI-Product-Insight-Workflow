Google AI Mode: Convenience vs. Trust

When search, recommendations, and transactions merge, trust becomes the product.

Google AI Mode

最有价值的动作：Compresses search, comparison, and booking into one conversation
仍然缺少什么：No clear labeling of commercial vs. organic recommendations

Traditional OTA

最有价值的动作：Transparent filters and sorting options
仍然缺少什么：Multiple tabs and repeated inputs
